# Frames

A frame is a live preview of a workspace's dev server. It's a real browser
view, so hot reload, client-side routing, and app state all work as normal.

<Screenshot
  name="frame-selected"
  alt="A selected frame with its resize handles and floating toolbar"
/>

## Adding frames

- A new workspace gets a frame automatically once its dev server is up.
- The **Frame** tool (<kbd>F</kbd>) drops a 1280×800 frame where you click,
  or draws one where you drag. As you let go, the frame asks what it should
  show. Type what to build and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Shift</kbd>
  <kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line). The frame's size is sent along as the
  viewport, so a phone-sized frame gets a mobile layout. A frame shows a
  workspace, and a workspace needs a repository, so on a canvas with no
  [repository](/guides/repositories) the Frame tool is off until you add one.
- The chip beside **Send** says who answers. With nothing selected it's
  **New chat**: a new chat starts on it, and its workspace boots in that
  frame. If you had a frame, mockup or document selected when you drew, it's
  that layer's workspace instead: the frame shows that workspace, and your
  message goes to its chat, as if you'd sent it there (see
  [Sending while the agent works](/guides/agent#sending-while-the-agent-works)).
  Click the chip to switch between **New chat** and any workspace.

  <Screenshot
    name="frame-ask-answerer"
    alt="A frame drawn with the Home frame selected: the chip names the Home frame's workspace, and its menu lists New chat and every workspace"
  />

- Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click elsewhere to skip the question. The frame
  stays and shows **No Workspace**. Click **Start a chat** in it to ask again,
  or pick a workspace for it from its title (**Choose a workspace**).

  <Screenshot
    name="frame-ask"
    alt="A frame just drawn, asking what it should show, with the New chat chip beside the Send button"
  />

  <Screenshot
    name="frame-unanswered"
    alt="A frame with no workspace, offering Start a chat"
  />

- **Show all routes** in the workspace menu adds a group with a frame for
  every route the workspace has visited.

## The title bar

Above each frame:

- **Name**: double-click to rename.
- **Workspace**, with its state icon, unless its
  [group](/guides/canvas#a-groups-workspace) names it because every frame in
  the group shows the same one. Click it to switch the frame's workspace.

Under the selected frame, an address bar shows its workspace and route. Click
the workspace to point this frame at another one, keeping its route and
state; this is how you compare workspaces on the same screen. Picking one
that differs from the other frames in its group moves the workspace from the
group's title onto each frame's. Click the route to edit
it in place, like a browser's address bar: type any path and press **Enter**, or
pick a route the workspace has visited from the list under the bar. **Esc**
puts the route back. Routes you visit while interacting
are added to the list automatically.

The route and scroll position are shared: when anyone viewing the canvas
navigates a frame, everyone else's copy follows. Your copy changes page
through the app's own router (Next's router, or the history API for React
Router, Vite and similar apps), so anything you typed or opened in the frame
stays put. Only a page with no client-side router reloads onto the new route.

## The frame toolbar

When exactly one frame is selected, a toolbar like a browser's appears under
it. From left to right:

| Button                | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Back**, **Forward** | Step through the routes this frame has shown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Address**           | The frame's Workspace and route. Click the route to type a new one. **Reload** reloads the preview, and is highlighted when hot reload has disconnected.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Record flow**       | Like Interact, but every time you navigate, a snapshot of the previous page is left behind in the group. Clicking through a signup therefore lays the whole flow out as a row of screens. The address turns red with a screen count while recording.                                                                                                  |
| **Interact**          | Lets the frame take your clicks and scrolls, so you can use the app. The canvas stops panning over it, though <kbd>Space</kbd>-drag outside it still pans. Double-clicking a frame does the same. The button and the frame's outline turn pink, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> (even from inside the preview) or a click outside the frame returns to the canvas. |
| **Knobs**             | Live controls your app declares. See [Knobs](#knobs).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **More**              | **Device size**, **Fit to content**, **Duplicate**, a **Workspace** submenu with **Open prototype player** and **Open in browser**, and **Delete frame**.                                                                                                                                                                                             |

### Who's driving

One party drives a frame at a time, and only the driver's clicks and keys
reach it. Interact is the driver's seat: pressing it makes you the driver,
and leaving it lets go.

When Claude drives a frame, the frame shows it:

- **Claude is driving** sits on the frame's title line, at its right edge.
- An unselected frame gets a 2px ring, so you can spot it
  from across the canvas.
- Selected, the Interact button shows Claude's dots instead of the cursor, and
  the frame has no resize handles, so its size can't change under Claude.

### Shared frames

On a hosted canvas, a frame is one browser running in its workspace's
sandbox, and everyone who has the frame on screen watches that same browser.
Two people looking at a frame see the same page in the same state, and when
one of them clicks through it the others watch it happen live.

Whoever drives the frame is the only one whose clicks, keys and scrolling
reach it. Press Interact on a frame nobody drives to pick it up. On a frame
someone else drives, nothing you do reaches the page: its title line says who
is driving, and the Interact button shows their avatar.

#### Asking for control

To take a turn on a frame someone else drives, click the Interact button
(its tooltip says **Click to ask for control**). The person driving sees your
ask under their Interact button and picks **Let drive** or **Not now**. When
they let you drive, the frame is yours and you're interacting with it. Click
the button again to take your ask back.

<Screenshot
  name="frame-control-requests"
  alt="Under the Interact button, Ana asks to drive and Ben asks to drive, each with Not now and Let drive"
/>

When several people ask at once, the driver sees each of them and picks one;
the others keep waiting. When the driver leaves Interact, control passes to
whoever asked first. A driver who reloads the page or drops their connection
keeps control for 5 seconds. If they don't come back, control passes to
whoever asked first and is still here, or to nobody.

Each frame streams at up to twice its size, so it stays sharp when you zoom in
to 200%. When a workspace's sandbox goes to sleep, its frames' browsers stop
with it; when it wakes up, each frame reopens at the page it was on.

Comment pins, Knobs, the element picker and **Fit to content** work on a
shared frame as on any other. A pin sits on the same element for everyone, and
a Knob you change shows in the one shared page, so everyone sees it at once.

The desktop app keeps its own frames: there, every frame is a preview running
on your Mac.

## Sizes

**More → Device size** sets exact device dimensions:

<Screenshot
  name="device-size-menu"
  alt="The Device size menu grouped into Desktop, Tablet and Mobile"
/>

| Group   | Presets                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Desktop | 4K (3840×2160), 1920×1080, MacBook Pro 16″ (1728×1117), MacBook Pro 14″ (1512×982), Laptop 1440×900, Laptop 1280×800                                    |
| Tablet  | iPad Pro 13″, iPad Pro 11″, iPad Air 13″, iPad Air 11″, iPad mini, Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra                                                                 |
| Mobile  | iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone SE, Pixel 9 Pro XL, Pixel 9 Pro, Galaxy S25 Ultra, Galaxy S25, Galaxy Z Fold (unfolded) |

You can also drag a corner handle. While resizing, the frame **snaps** to
nearby device sizes, which are outlined as you get close. Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd>
to resize freely. **Fit to content** resizes the frame to the page's full
document size, which is handy for capturing a whole long page.

A repository's **Default frame size** decides what new frames open at.

## Knobs

If your app declares [knobs](/building/knobs), the **Knobs** button opens live
controls: sliders, switches, text fields, selects, and color pickers. Changes
apply instantly and sync to everyone viewing the canvas. A dot on the button
means values differ from their defaults, and **Reset** in its header clears
them.

<Screenshot
  name="knobs-popover"
  alt="The Knobs popover with Accent color, Corner radius, Headline, Show customer logos and Hero layout"
/>

With no knobs declared, the popover explains what knobs are and offers **Ask
the agent to add a knob**, which opens the workspace's chat with a prompt
filled in.

## Shared state

When your app shares state through
[`@screenplay.space/state`](/building/shared-state), a small `{}` appears on
the route in the selected frame's address bar. Hover it to see the current JSON. The state is kept with the
frame and synced to everyone.

## Preview states

While there's no live page, a frame shows where its workspace is:

- **No Workspace** until you pick one for the frame.
- **Booting sandbox**, then **Starting dev server**, while the workspace boots.
- **Workspace failed to start** if setup failed, and **Dev server not
  responding** if the server doesn't answer in time. Both offer **Retry** and
  **Open logs**.
- **Workspace stopped** when its sandbox is stopped. Click **Start**.

<Callout type="info">
  A preview that never loads on desktop usually means the dev server isn't
  listening on the port Screenplay assigned. See [Dev server &
  ports](/building/dev-server).
</Callout>
