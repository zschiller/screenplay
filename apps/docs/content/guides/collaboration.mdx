# Sharing & collaboration

<Callout type="info">
  Everything on this page is part of the **hosted** app. The desktop app is
  single-user: it has no sharing, cursors, or stored comments. You can still
  send a document selection to the agent; see
  [Documents](/guides/documents#sending-a-selection-to-the-agent).
</Callout>

A canvas is a shared, live space. Everyone in it sees the same frames,
documents, workspaces, and agent chats, with changes appearing as they
happen. If a teammate asks the agent for something, you watch it stream in
too.

## Sharing a canvas

As the owner, click **Share** in the canvas's top right, or **Share** in the
canvas menu on the home screen. Invite people by the email address of their
Screenplay account. **People with access** lists everyone; remove someone
with the trash icon.

There are two roles:

- **Owner** can rename, share, and delete the canvas.
- **Collaborators** can do everything else: add projects and workspaces, chat
  with the agent, and edit the canvas. They can pin the canvas and file it
  into their own folders. Their **Delete** is **Leave**.

There's no link sharing: people need access to be invited.

## Seeing each other

- **Cursors.** Everyone's pointer shows on the canvas with their name, in
  their color. Selections are outlined in that color too.
- **Avatars.** The top right shows who's here. Click an avatar to **follow**
  that person: your view tracks their viewport as they pan and zoom. Click
  again, or your own avatar, to stop.
- **Cursor chat.** Press <kbd>/</kbd> to type a short message (up to 140
  characters) that floats next to your cursor for everyone to see.
  <kbd>↵</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

Commits are made under the name of whoever sent the message, so git history
stays accurate on a shared canvas.

## Comments

Press <kbd>C</kbd> (or pick **Comment** in the toolbar):

- **On a frame**, hover to outline the element under the pointer, then click
  to pin a comment to it. The pin stays attached to that element.
- **On a document**, select text to comment on it. The comment quotes the text
  and its line numbers.

Type your comment and click **Comment** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>↵</kbd>). For
document comments, **Send to agent** turns the note and quoted text into a
new agent chat instead.

Pins show the author's avatar and are highlighted when unread. Open a pin to
reply, **Resolve thread**, **Mark as unread**, or delete. The comment button in
the top bar shows how many threads are open, with a dot when some are unread;
pick a thread from its list to open it and pan to its pin. In
[play mode](/guides/play-mode), **Comments** shows a simple per-workspace
feed.

## Terminal tabs stay private

[Terminal tabs](/guides/terminals) belong to the person who opened them and
aren't visible to collaborators.
