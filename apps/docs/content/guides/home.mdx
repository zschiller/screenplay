# Home & organizing

The home screen lists your canvases. Each card's thumbnail is assembled from
the canvas's own frames, so you can recognize a canvas at a glance. Frames
still starting up show as blocks in their workspace's color.

<Screenshot name="home-recents" alt="The Recents view: a grid of canvas cards with live thumbnails, and Pinned items in the sidebar" />

## Views

The sidebar has three destinations:

- **Recents** lists every canvas you can open, most recently edited first,
  regardless of folder.
- **All files** is your folder tree: folders first, then canvases. Click a
  folder to open it. A breadcrumb shows where you are, and deep paths collapse
  into a **…** menu.
- **Settings**: see [Settings](/guides/settings).

Below those, **Pinned** lists your shortcuts (see [Pinning](#pinning)).

<Screenshot name="home-folder" alt="Inside the Marketing folder, with the All files › Marketing breadcrumb" />

### Grid and table

Switch between **Grid view** and **Table view** with the toggle at the top
right. The choice applies to every page. The table shows **Name**, **Last
edited** and **Created**, plus **Owner** on the hosted app. Click a column
header to sort by it; click again to reverse.

<Screenshot name="home-table" alt="Table view with Name, Last edited and Created columns" />

### Sorting

In **All files** and in folders, the **sort** menu sorts by **Last edited**,
**Date created** or **Name**, in either order. Each folder remembers its own
sort. **Recents** is always sorted by last edit.

<Screenshot name="home-sort-menu" alt="The sort menu: Sort by Last edited, Date created or Name; Order Newest or Oldest first" />

## Creating

- **New canvas** asks for a name, then opens the new canvas. Leave the name
  blank to get "Untitled".
- **Add folder** creates a folder inside the folder you're viewing.

## Canvas and folder menus

Hover a card or row and click **…**:

<Screenshot name="home-canvas-menu" alt="The canvas menu: Rename, Move to…, Pin to sidebar, Delete" />

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Renames the canvas. Only the owner can rename a shared canvas. |
| **Move to…** | Files it into a folder, or back to **All files**. Shown once you have a folder. |
| **Pin to sidebar** / **Unpin** | Adds or removes the sidebar shortcut. |
| **Share** | *Hosted only, owner only.* See [Sharing](/guides/collaboration#sharing-a-canvas). |
| **Delete** / **Leave** | Owners delete; people it's shared with leave. |

Folders have the same menu, minus Share.

## Filing

**Move to…** opens a picker of your folders. You can also **drag** a canvas or
folder onto:

- a folder card or row,
- a pinned folder in the sidebar,
- **All files** in the sidebar, which moves it to the top level.

Folders nest to any depth. Screenplay stops you from moving a folder into
itself or one of its own subfolders.

<Screenshot name="home-folder-menu" alt="The folder menu: Rename, Move to…, Unpin, Delete" />

<Callout type="info">
  Folders and filing are **personal**. When someone shares a canvas with you,
  it appears at the top level of your All files. Filing it only changes your
  view; the owner's organization is untouched.
</Callout>

## Pinning

**Pin to sidebar** adds a canvas or folder to the **Pinned** section for quick
access. Pinning never moves anything, and unpinning leaves the item where it
was. Drag pinned rows to reorder them. Pins are personal too.

<Screenshot name="home-all-files" alt="All files with the Product and Marketing folders, and a pinned canvas and folder in the sidebar" />

## Deleting

- **Deleting a canvas** permanently removes it and everything on it. If you
  shared it, it's removed for everyone, and the dialog tells you how many
  people that affects.
- **Leaving a canvas** someone shared with you only removes your access. The
  owner can invite you again.
- **Deleting a folder** deletes everything inside it, including sub-folders
  and the canvases you own. Canvases shared *with* you that you had filed
  there aren't deleted; you just leave them. The dialog lists what will be
  affected before you confirm.
