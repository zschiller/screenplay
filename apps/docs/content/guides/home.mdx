# Home & organizing

The home screen lists your canvases. Each card's thumbnail is assembled from
the canvas's own frames, so you can recognize a canvas at a glance. Frames
still starting up show as plain blocks.

/>

## Views

The sidebar has two views at the top:

- **Recents** lists every canvas you can open, most recently edited first,
  regardless of folder.
- **All files** is your folder tree: folders first, then canvases. Click a
  folder to open it. A breadcrumb shows where you are, and deep paths collapse
  into a **…** menu.

Below those, **Pinned** lists your shortcuts (see [Pinning](#pinning)). At the
foot of the sidebar, **Docs** opens these docs in your browser, and
**Settings** opens [Settings](/guides/settings).
**Search** at the top of the sidebar (<kbd>/</kbd>) finds canvases and folders
across all folders. Matches open in a list under the field, each with where it
lives; use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to pick one and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to
open it. On the hosted app, the owner filter in the toolbar (**Anyone**,
**Owned by me**, **Shared with me**) lists matches from every folder on the
page, each with the folder it lives in (a **Location** column in the table,
in place of **Created**).

### Grid and table

Switch between **Grid view** and **Table view** with the toggle at the top
right. The choice applies to every page. The table shows **Name**, **Last
edited** and **Created**, plus **Owner** on the hosted app. Click a column
header to sort by it; click again to reverse.

<Screenshot
  name="home-table"
  alt="Table view with Name, Last edited and Created columns"
/>

### Sorting

Sort from the table's column headers. The grid keeps the order you last picked
in the table, and each page (Recents, All files, each folder) remembers its
own. Until you pick one, everything is sorted by last edit, newest first.

## Creating

- **New canvas** (or <kbd>N</kbd>) creates an "Untitled" canvas and opens it
  straight away. Rename it from the canvas's title pill. A folder's **…** menu
  also has **New canvas**, which creates it inside that folder.
- **New folder** creates a folder inside the folder you're viewing.

## Canvas and folder menus

Hover a card or row and click **…**:

<Screenshot
  name="home-canvas-menu"
  alt="The canvas menu: Rename, Move to…, Pin to sidebar, Delete"
/>

| Item                           | What it does                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rename**                     | Renames the canvas. Only the owner can rename a shared canvas.                     |
| **Move to…**                   | Files it into a folder, or back to **All files**. Shown once you have a folder.    |
| **Pin to sidebar** / **Unpin** | Adds or removes the sidebar shortcut.                                              |
| **Share**                      | <Only hosted /> Owner only. See [Sharing](/guides/collaboration#sharing-a-canvas). |
| **Delete** / **Leave**         | Owners delete; people it's shared with leave.                                      |

Folders have the same menu, minus Share and plus **New canvas**.

## Filing

**Move to…** opens a picker of your folders, with **New folder** to make one
on the way. You can also **drag** a canvas or folder onto:

- a folder card or row,
- a pinned folder in the sidebar,
- **All files** in the sidebar, which moves it to the top level,
- a folder, or **All files**, in the breadcrumb.

Each move shows a toast with **Undo**.

Folders nest to any depth. Screenplay stops you from moving a folder into
itself or one of its own subfolders.

<Callout type="info">
  Folders and filing are **personal**. When someone shares a canvas with you, it
  appears at the top level of your All files. Filing it only changes your view;
  the owner's organization is untouched.
</Callout>

## Pinning

**Pin to sidebar** adds a canvas or folder to the **Pinned** section for quick
access. Pinning never moves anything, and unpinning leaves the item where it
was. Drag pinned rows to reorder them. Pins are personal too.

## Deleting

- **Deleting a canvas** permanently removes it and everything on it. If you
  shared it, it's removed for everyone, and the dialog tells you how many
  people that affects.
- **Leaving a canvas** someone shared with you only removes your access. The
  owner can invite you again.
- **Deleting a folder** deletes everything inside it, including sub-folders
  and the canvases you own. Canvases shared _with_ you that you had filed
  there aren't deleted; you just leave them. The dialog lists what will be
  affected before you confirm.
