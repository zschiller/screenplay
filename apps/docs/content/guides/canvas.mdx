# The canvas

A canvas has three regions:

- **Left sidebar** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>): the canvas’s layers, its
  groups, frames and documents.
- **The canvas itself**: an infinite surface of frames and documents, with
  the toolbar at the bottom center.
- **Agent panel** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>): the [Coordinator](/guides/coordinator), or chat and
  terminals for the selected workspace. Its **Chats** button
  lists every chat (see
  [The Chats menu](/guides/workspaces#the-chats-menu)).

The shortcuts hide and show the side panels. While one is hidden, **Show
sidebar** at the top left or **Show chat** at the top right brings it back.

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd> hides both panels at once, or brings them back. Drag a
panel’s edge to resize it; the layout is remembered.

## Moving around

| Action                | How                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pan                   | Scroll or trackpad-swipe, or hold <kbd>Space</kbd> and drag                                              |
| Zoom                  | <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + scroll, or pinch; <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>=</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>-</kbd> |
| Zoom to 100% / to fit | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd> / <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd>                                                    |
| Jump to a layer       | Double-click it in the sidebar’s layer list                                                              |

The zoom percentage at the top right opens the zoom menu: **Zoom in**,
**Zoom out**, **Zoom to fit**, **Zoom to 50%**, **Zoom to 100%**, **Zoom to
200%**, and **Keyboard shortcuts** (also <kbd>?</kbd>). Your viewport is saved
with the canvas, so you come back to where you left off.

## The top bar

The pill at the top left shows where the canvas lives: **All files** or its
folder, then the canvas name. Double-click the name to rename it. The **…**
menu has **Rename**, **Settings** (the canvas’s
[repositories](/guides/repositories), [memory](/guides/memory#canvas-memory),
[files](/guides/files) and [skills](/guides/building/skills#saving-a-skill-to-the-canvas))
and **Delete**. On a canvas shared with you, only the owner can rename it, so
the menu has **Settings** and **Leave**.

The top right holds the zoom menu. On the hosted app it also shows the
comment count, who else is here, and **Share**. See [Sharing & collaboration](/guides/collaboration).

## Tools

The toolbar at the bottom holds the drawing tools. Exactly one is active at a
time. Pressing a tool’s key again returns to **Select**.

| Tool         | Key          | Use                                                                               |
| ------------ | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Select**   | <kbd>V</kbd> | Select, move and resize layers                                                    |
| **Frame**    | <kbd>F</kbd> | Click to drop a 1280×800 frame, or drag to draw one, then say what it should show |
| **Mockup**   | <kbd>M</kbd> | Click or drag to place a [mockup](/guides/mockups), then say what to sketch in it |
| **Document** | <kbd>D</kbd> | Click to drop a document, or drag to draw one                                     |
| **Comment**  | <kbd>C</kbd> | <Only hosted /> Pin a comment to an element                                       |

While the Frame or Document tool is active, every group shows a dashed
**+** slot at its end. Click it to add the new layer to that group. Adding a
layer, by clicking, dragging or using a slot, selects it and returns to
**Select**.

## Selecting

- **Click** a layer’s title bar to select it. **Shift-click** adds to or
  removes from the selection.
- **Drag** on empty canvas to draw a selection marquee.
- Clicking a group’s label selects the whole group.
- <kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>Backspace</kbd> deletes the selection, and the next
  layer is selected so you can keep going.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> duplicates the selected frames and mockups, adding
  each copy at the end of its group and selecting the copies.
- With more than one thing selected, groups included, toolbars and menus
  hide so nothing acts on just one of them.

## Layer menus

Each frame, mockup, document and group has one menu, and it’s the same
wherever you open it: **…** on its row in the [layers list](#the-layers-list),
**More** on the toolbar under a selected frame or mockup, or **…** after the
name of a selected document or group. It holds, as they apply:

- **Rename** and **Duplicate** (frames and mockups).
- A frame’s **Device size**, **Fit to content** and a **Chat** submenu with
  its [chat menu](/guides/workspaces#workspace-actions).
- **Delete**.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> backs out one step at a time: it closes whatever is open
  (editing, a tool, interact mode), then clears the selection.

## Groups

Layers on the canvas always live in **groups**: rows of frames, documents and
[mockups](/guides/mockups) laid out left to right with an even gap. Groups keep related screens together,
such as a page’s desktop and mobile versions or the steps of a signup flow.

- **Move a group** by dragging any member’s title bar or the group label.
  Edges and centers snap to nearby groups, with guide lines.
- **Merge groups** by dragging one group onto the end of another; the target
  lights up. Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> to move without merging.
- **Reorder within a group** by selecting the group, then dragging a member
  along the row. Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> while dragging to pull it out into a new
  group.
- **Change spacing** by selecting the group and dragging the handles between
  members.
- **Rename** a group by double-clicking its label, or from its **…** menu
  in the sidebar.

A group disappears when its last member is removed.

### A group’s workspace

When every frame in a group shows the same workspace, as the screens of one
flow do, the group names it after the group’s name: its state icon (a spinner
while it sets up, moving dots while its agent works), its title, and its pull
request when there’s room. Its frames leave the workspace off their own titles;
to show one of them from another workspace, hover the frame’s name and click
**Set workspace** after it.

When the frames show different workspaces, as parallel explorations do, the
group names none, and each frame’s title ends with its own workspace. Switching
one frame to another workspace turns a flow into such a group; switching it back
turns it into a flow again. To put every frame in such a group on one
workspace, hover the group’s name and click **Set workspace** after it. A group
with a single frame has no group label, so that frame’s title names the
workspace instead.

To show a flow from another workspace, click the group’s workspace and pick one
from the list (for a group named Checkout, it reads **Show Checkout from…**).
Every frame in the group moves, each keeping its route, state and size.
Documents in the group are unaffected. The switch is one undo step, and
everyone on the canvas sees it at once.

A group whose frames have no workspace yet shows **Choose a workspace** after
its name instead, and its frames show No workspace, with **Start a chat** to
build something new in them, until you pick. Picking one
sets the group and all of its frames. Once some frames have a workspace and
others don’t, each frame offers its own list. A group of documents only has no
workspace: when its last frame is moved out, split off or deleted, the group
drops its workspace, and the next frame to join sets it.

## The layers list

The sidebar’s layer list mirrors the canvas. Groups with several
members appear as folders. A flow’s row ends with its workspace (state icon
and title, with its pull request at the row’s end), and its frame rows show
just their names. In a group whose frames differ, and in single-frame groups,
each frame row ends with its own workspace instead.

- Click to select; <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click to add to the selection.
- Double-click to zoom to it.
- Drag rows to reorder, move a layer into another group, or drop it between
  groups to start a new one.
- **…** opens the layer’s [menu](#layer-menus), the same one as on the
  canvas. A delete happens at once; <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> brings it back.

## Undo

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> undo and
redo your own canvas changes: moving, resizing, adding, deleting and renaming
frames, documents and groups. A frame’s page, its route and scroll, isn’t part
of undo. Your ⌘Z never undoes someone else’s changes or the Coordinator’s.

Deleting a frame, document or group happens at once, whether from the layers
list, the frame toolbar or the <kbd>Delete</kbd> key, with no confirm and no
toast; <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> brings it back. Everyone on the canvas sees
the delete, and the undo. Deleting a
workspace or a repository still asks first, because undo can’t bring those
back.

<Callout type="info">
  Undo covers the canvas layout only. It doesn’t touch code: to reverse
  something the agent changed, ask it, or use git.
</Callout>
