Who drives the live frame?

One copy of a frame's prototype runs for the whole room. One person drives it at a time: only their clicks and typing reach it, and everyone else watches. The room record (in the room's Yjs doc) says who drives. Anyone else can ask to drive, like screen share in Zoom.

Try the questions from the ticket: does the driver hand over or does the asker take over, what happens when the driver closes the tab, what do two requests at once do, what does a watcher see, and does it hold for both the streamed and the mirrored route.
