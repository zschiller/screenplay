Unbounded, a size smaller

You picked Unbounded and asked for a smaller type scale so it stays oversized, just not that oversized. The Unbounded sizes tab shows every serif title (page titles, dialog and Settings titles, Document title, H1 and H2) at 100%, 85%, 75% and 65% of today’s sizes, with the UI on Instrument Sans. Each option lists its pixel sizes. Following your note, Unbounded tapered scales the biggest titles to 75% and the smallest to 85% (27, 24, 20 and 17px). That’s my pick now.

Round 6

Round 6 follows your round 5 notes. Archivo Expanded was the favourite but its UI sans was too high contrast, so every option now keeps Instrument Sans for the UI and changes only the titles. It opens on the Wide and weird tab. Six grotesks sit in Archivo’s direction (Space Grotesk, Unbounded, Syne, Sora, Anybody, Funnel Display). Five get weird: a Cooper-style serif (Caprasimo), three other condensed serifs (Gloock, DM Serif Display, Noto Serif Display Condensed) and one oddball (Tilt Warp). My pick is Syne: it’s wide like Archivo but has more character. If you’d rather go weird, my pick there is Caprasimo.

Round 5

Round 5 replaces Instrument Serif with faces outside the conventional text serifs from round 4 (Newsreader, Source Serif, Fraunces, Libre Caslon and Young Serif are not repeated). Two are serifs that aren’t condensed (Bodoni Moda, Besley), two are grotesks (Bricolage Grotesque, and Archivo Expanded, which also swaps the UI sans), and one is a mono (Martian Mono). Everything else is today’s app on main: Accent, Phosphor Light, Geist Mono labels. The screens are real captures with only the fonts injected; “Hold to show today” flips back to Instrument Serif. The live homepage hero comes right after the viewer. My pick is Bodoni Moda, with Archivo Expanded as the bold alternative.
