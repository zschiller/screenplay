Shared rules behind the mockups

Most of what looks off comes from a few missing rules, not from any one screen. These apply to every ticket above.

  • Identity is never status. Workspace colours come from a palette of indigo, cyan, violet, slate, pink and teal. Red, amber and green mean status only (#718).
  • Fills are rare. A solid fill marks the one primary action or the one selected item. Names, branches and user messages go back to plain text.
  • One border per object. Group related rows inside one bordered container with dividers, instead of a card per row.
  • Two text sizes carry most of the UI: 13px for content and 12px muted for metadata. 14px, 16px and 20px are for dialog body, dialog titles and page titles only (#739).
  • Balanced centred copy with a max width, everywhere.
  • Floating chrome never covers content: toolbars, HUDs and popovers anchor beside what they belong to.
  • Labels beat icons where there's room: frame toolbar, player bar, settings actions.
