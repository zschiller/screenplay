Fewer font sizes, none below 12px

The app renders ten text sizes today, and the three smallest (10, 11 and 12.8px) do most of the damage: tool output, diff counts and section labels are hard to read. Below are three consolidated scales, each captured on real screens from the screenshot harness in light and dark. The Unbounded titles (27/24/20/17) stay as approved in all three.

On real screens

Pick a scale, then hold Hold for before (or press B) to flip back to main. Click a screen to see it at 1:1. These are live captures with each scale applied as a stylesheet over main, so heights and truncation are real, but nothing has been retuned yet.

A · 12/14

Recommendation

Go with A. It removes 10, 11 and 12.8px and fixes the too-small text without touching anything that already reads well. It's also the smallest change: delete the text-3xs and text-2xs tokens, move their 50 uses to text-xs, put the sm Button on text-sm, and the type-scale test can then reject the two retired tokens too.

B is the pick if the whole app feels small, not just the metadata. C is the most tailored but adds a step and moves away from stock shadcn, which you've preferred elsewhere.

Whichever you pick, a follow-up pass retunes the few spots that got tight: tool-row truncation in chat, the diff counts beside Workspace names, and the frame address bar.
