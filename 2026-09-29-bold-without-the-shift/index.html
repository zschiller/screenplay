Bold without the shift

The active sidebar row, the current Settings section and the current getting-started step switch from 400 to 500 weight. Instrument Sans gets wider when it gets bolder, so the label grows by 0.4 to 2.2px and anything beside it moves. Every mock below cycles its active item so you can watch for it.

Baseline

Today

Shifts

Watch the Settings sections: when “Coding agents” or “Repository presets” becomes current, the sections after it slide right, and a row can wrap.

Option A

Keep Instrument Sans, stop bolding

No shift
How: drop font-medium from the active state in three places. The fill alone marks the active item. Cost: a quieter active state, and a step away from stock shadcn, whose sidebar bolds the active row.
Option B

Keep Instrument Sans, narrow it as it bolds

Recommended
How: Instrument Sans has a width axis. Active items get 500 weight at 98.8% width, which cancels the growth to within 0.4px. We load the width axis and add font-stretch-[98.8%] next to each active font-medium. Cost: almost none. The bold looks the same as today, and the leftover sub-pixel difference doesn't move anything visibly.
Option C

Switch to a uniwidth font (Recursive Sans)

No shift
How: Recursive in its Sans Linear style keeps every glyph the same width at every weight, so any weight change is free, everywhere. Cost: it looks different from Instrument Sans (rounder, larger x-height, about 8% wider), and it is the only free uniwidth face close to this style. The other ones that look like Instrument Sans are paid.
