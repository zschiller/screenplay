Voice and terms

A short guide the rewrites below follow. It builds on apps/app/CONTEXT.md, which already names what each concept is called in the UI.

  • Plain words. Say what happens, from the person's side. No mechanism words (inject, bind, glob, checkout, build) unless the field is about that mechanism.
  • Errors say what went wrong and what to do. Lead with “Couldn't …”, never “Failed to …” or “Unknown error”. Point at a control that exists.
  • Contractions (can't, isn't, doesn't). No “please”, no “cannot”.
  • No em-dashes in UI copy. Two short sentences instead.
  • One verb per action. A button's verb is the toast's verb (Delete, then “Deleted”). Pairs match: Show and Hide, not Open and Hide.
  • Full labels. Never shorten a label to fit a menu or button; widen the menu instead.
  • Sentence case for labels, titles and menu items.
  • Empty states say why or what to do next, not only what is missing.
  • “Agent” for the AI in every UI string, never “Claude”. Claude Code and Codex appear only where someone picks which coding agent to install.
