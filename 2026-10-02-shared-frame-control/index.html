Rules every option follows

  • One driver per shared frame. People ask, and the driver lets them drive (#981). Watchers' clicks never reach the page.
  • The agent asks through the same gate but always yields: anyone takes control from it instantly, on the Mac too (#1370).
  • Asking the agent in chat to show you something hands it control.
  • Going to your own local copy is for you alone. Rejoining discards your copy (#1369).
  • Hosted frames are shared; the Mac has one local iframe and nobody else.
